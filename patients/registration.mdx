Patient registration is the starting point for every care journey in eHealthSystem. Before a patient can receive treatment, schedule an appointment, or have lab results recorded, a patient record must exist in the system. Registering a patient correctly — with accurate demographic, contact, and insurance details — ensures that every downstream workflow, from billing to prescriptions, runs without interruption.

## Who Can Register Patients

Any user assigned the **Receptionist** role can register new patients by default. Users with other roles — such as Nurse, Clinical Officer, or Administrator — can also register patients if their account has been granted the **Registration** permission by a system administrator. If you don't see the registration option in your sidebar, contact your system administrator to review your role permissions.

## Registration Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Patient Form">
    In the left sidebar, navigate to **Patients** > **Register New Patient**. The patient registration form opens in the main content area. If you are already inside the Patient Management module, you can also click the **+ New Patient** button in the top-right corner of the patient list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Personal Details">
    Fill in the required personal information:
    - **First Name** and **Last Name**
    - **Date of Birth** — use the date picker or type in `DD/MM/YYYY` format
    - **Gender** — select from the dropdown (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say)
    - **National ID / Passport Number** (if your facility collects this)

    All fields marked with a red asterisk (`*`) are mandatory and must be completed before you can save the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Contact Information">
    Scroll to the **Contact Details** section and provide:
    - **Primary Phone Number** — used for appointment reminders and follow-up communications
    - **Secondary Phone Number** (optional)
    - **Email Address** (optional)
    - **Physical Address** — street, city, and region or county
    - **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone Number** — these are critical for inpatient and surgical cases
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Insurance or Payment Method">
    If the patient has health insurance, expand the **Insurance & Payment** section and complete:
    - **Insurance Provider** — select from the registered schemes or type to search
    - **Member Number / Policy Number**
    - **Scheme / Plan Name**
    - **Coverage Validity Dates**

    If the patient is self-paying or sponsored by an employer, select the appropriate **Payment Method** from the dropdown. You can update this information later from the patient record if the details are not available at registration time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Save">
    Scroll back through the form and verify that all information is accurate. When you are satisfied, click **Register Patient** (or **Save**, depending on your facility's configuration). eHealthSystem validates all required fields before saving. If any required field is missing or incorrectly formatted, an inline error message highlights the relevant field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the Generated Patient ID">
    After a successful save, eHealthSystem automatically generates a unique **Patient ID** (for example, `PT-2024-004821`). This ID appears prominently in the confirmation banner and at the top of the new patient record. Note it down or share it with the patient — it is used across all modules including appointments, billing, pharmacy, and lab results to uniquely identify this individual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Duplicate Patient Check

To prevent the same patient from being registered more than once, eHealthSystem automatically checks for potential duplicates when you enter a name and date of birth. If the system detects an existing record with a similar name and matching or close date of birth, a **Possible Duplicate Found** warning appears above the form before you save.

The warning displays the matching record(s) with their Patient IDs and basic details. Review the listed records carefully:
- If one of the listed patients is the same person, click **View Record** to open their existing profile and update any missing information instead of creating a new record.
- If the listed patients are genuinely different individuals, click **Continue Registration** to proceed and save the new record.

Registering a duplicate patient can cause split medical histories and billing issues, so always resolve duplicate warnings before saving.

<Note>
  Verify the patient's identity — using a national ID, passport, or other government-issued document — before completing registration. Accurate identification prevents record mix-ups, protects patient safety, and ensures correct billing. Your facility's patient identification policy takes precedence; consult your supervisor if you are unsure which documents to request.
</Note>

## After Registration

Once a patient is successfully registered, their record is immediately available across all modules in eHealthSystem:

- **Appointments** — schedule visits directly from the patient record
- **Clinical** — clinicians can open the record to document consultations and issue prescriptions
- **Laboratory** — lab technicians can attach test requests and results to the patient
- **Pharmacy** — pharmacists can view and dispense prescriptions linked to the patient
- **Billing** — finance staff can generate invoices and process payments against the patient account

Navigate to **Patients** > **Patient Management** to search for and open any registered patient record at any time.
