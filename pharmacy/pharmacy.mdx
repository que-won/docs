The Pharmacy module in eHealthSystem centralizes all medication dispensing workflows and inventory management for your facility. Pharmacists and dispensing staff use this module to receive prescriptions from clinicians, verify patient identity, confirm stock availability, dispense medications, and maintain an accurate drug inventory — all within a single, audited environment. Every dispensing action is timestamped and linked to the prescribing clinician's order, creating a complete medication trail for each patient.

---

## Viewing Incoming Prescriptions

To access the pharmacy dispensing queue:

1. Select **Pharmacy** from the main sidebar.
2. The **Dispensing Queue** loads automatically, displaying all active prescriptions awaiting fulfillment.

Each entry in the queue shows the following information at a glance:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Patient Name** | Full name of the patient for whom the prescription was written |
| **Medication(s)** | Drug name(s), dosage, and quantity prescribed |
| **Prescribing Doctor** | The clinician who issued the prescription |
| **Date / Time** | When the prescription was created or sent to pharmacy |

Prescriptions are color-coded by status: **Pending** (yellow), **On Hold** (orange), **Dispensed** (green), and **Rejected** (red). Use the filter bar at the top of the queue to narrow results by status, date range, or prescribing doctor.

## Dispensing a Prescription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the prescription in the dispensing queue">
    Find the patient's prescription in the queue. You can use the search bar to look up a patient by name, patient ID, or prescription number. Prescriptions are sorted by arrival time by default — most recent at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the prescription details">
    Click on the prescription row to open the full details panel. This view shows each medication item, the prescribed dosage and frequency, the prescribing doctor's notes, and any allergy flags or interaction warnings generated by the system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the patient's identity">
    Before dispensing, confirm the patient's identity by checking their **full name** and **patient ID number** against the prescription. If the patient is present at the counter, ask them to state their name and verify it against the record. Never dispense based on name alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check stock availability">
    The system displays real-time stock levels beside each medication item. A green indicator means sufficient stock is available. A red indicator means the item is out of stock or below the required quantity. Review each line item before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Dispense'">
    Once you have verified the patient's identity and confirmed stock availability for all items, click the **Dispense** button at the bottom of the prescription details panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter batch number and expiry date">
    If your facility tracks batch-level traceability (recommended for controlled substances and high-value medications), enter the **batch number** and **expiry date** from the physical packaging for each medication being dispensed. These fields are mandatory for medications flagged as controlled drugs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm dispensing">
    Click **Confirm Dispensing** to finalize the transaction. The prescription status updates to **Dispensed**, the stock count decrements automatically, and the patient's medication record is updated. The prescribing clinician also receives an in-app notification that the prescription has been fulfilled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Partial Dispensing

If some medications in a prescription are in stock but others are not, you can perform a partial dispense rather than holding the entire prescription.

1. Open the prescription and review each medication line item.
2. Deselect the checkboxes next to items that are **out of stock** or unavailable.
3. Click **Partial Dispense** — the system will dispense only the selected items.
4. The prescription status updates to **Partially Dispensed**, and the remaining items remain in the queue.
5. Add a note explaining which items were not dispensed and the reason (e.g., *"Amoxicillin 500mg out of stock — awaiting delivery"*).
6. When new stock arrives, return to the prescription from the queue (it remains active) and dispense the remaining items.

The patient's medication record clearly distinguishes which items were dispensed and which are pending, so clinical staff and the patient are always informed.

## Drug Inventory Management

### Viewing Current Stock Levels

Navigate to **Pharmacy → Inventory** to see a full list of all drugs in your facility's formulary. The inventory table displays the drug name, formulation, current quantity on hand, reorder level, and expiry status. Use the search bar or filter by drug category to locate specific items quickly.

### Adding New Stock (Receiving Deliveries)

When a delivery arrives:

1. Go to **Pharmacy → Inventory → Receive Stock**.
2. Select the drug from the formulary list or scan its barcode.
3. Enter the quantity received, batch number, manufacture date, and expiry date.
4. Select the supplier from the dropdown or add a new supplier if needed.
5. Click **Save** — the system adds the new quantity to the existing stock level and logs the transaction with the date, time, and your user account.

If a drug is not yet in your formulary, click **Add New Drug** and complete the drug profile (name, generic name, formulation, unit, category) before receiving stock.

### Setting Low-Stock Alerts

To configure a reorder alert for a drug:

1. Open the drug's record from the inventory list.
2. Click **Edit** and locate the **Reorder Level** field.
3. Enter the minimum quantity below which you want to receive an alert.
4. Click **Save**.

When stock falls at or below this threshold, the system displays a badge on the **Pharmacy** sidebar item and sends an alert to all pharmacy staff. You can also configure email notifications for low-stock events under **Settings → Notifications**.

### Expired Medication Management

The inventory dashboard flags medications whose expiry date falls within the next **30 days** (amber) or that have already expired (red). To manage expired stock:

1. Navigate to **Pharmacy → Inventory → Expired Drugs**.
2. Review the list of expired or near-expiry items.
3. Select the items to be removed from active inventory.
4. Click **Write Off** and select a reason (e.g., *Expired*, *Damaged*, *Recalled*).
5. Confirm the write-off. The system records the quantity removed, the reason, and your user account in the audit log.

Expired medications are removed from the dispensable stock immediately and can no longer be selected during a dispense action.

---

<Warning>
Always verify the patient's identity — by **full name and patient ID** — before dispensing any medication. Dispensing to the wrong patient is a serious clinical safety incident. If you have any doubt about a patient's identity, ask for a valid ID document before proceeding. Do not accept verbal confirmation alone for controlled or high-risk medications.
</Warning>

## Prescription Hold and Rejection

### Placing a Prescription on Hold

If you need to pause processing — for example, while waiting for a stock delivery, clarifying a dosage with the prescribing doctor, or pending insurance verification — place the prescription on hold:

1. Open the prescription from the queue.
2. Click **Place on Hold**.
3. Select a hold reason from the dropdown (e.g., *Awaiting Stock*, *Clarification Required*, *Insurance Pending*).
4. Add a free-text note if needed.
5. Click **Confirm Hold**.

The prescription status changes to **On Hold** and remains visible in the queue. The prescribing clinician receives a notification with the hold reason.

### Rejecting a Prescription

Reject a prescription only when it cannot be fulfilled and should be returned to the clinician for reissuance — for example, if the prescription contains a drug not in the formulary, the dosage is outside safe limits, or a critical allergy interaction is identified.

1. Open the prescription and click **Reject**.
2. Select a rejection reason from the dropdown.
3. Enter a detailed note explaining the rejection.
4. Click **Confirm Rejection**.

The prescribing clinician is notified immediately with your rejection reason. Rejected prescriptions are removed from the active queue and archived in the patient's medication history with a **Rejected** status.

## Dispensing Receipts

After successfully dispensing a prescription, you can generate a dispensing slip or medication label for the patient.

- **Dispensing Slip:** Click **Print Slip** from the confirmation screen or from the prescription details of any dispensed prescription. The slip includes the patient's name, medication(s) dispensed, dosage instructions, dispensing date, and the pharmacist's name.
- **Medication Label:** Click **Print Label** to generate adhesive labels for each medication package. Labels include the drug name, dose, frequency, patient name, and dispensing date. You can configure label templates under **Pharmacy → Settings → Label Templates**.
- **Digital Copy:** The dispensing record is automatically saved to the patient's profile. Patients who have the eHealthSystem patient portal enabled can view their medication history and download dispensing slips directly.

## Pharmacy Reports

For detailed pharmacy analytics — including dispensing volumes, stock movement, controlled drug registers, and revenue from medication sales — navigate to **Admin → Reports → Pharmacy Reports**. You can filter reports by date range, drug category, prescribing doctor, or patient. Reports can be exported as PDF or CSV for submission to regulatory bodies or internal audits.

<Tip>
Schedule automated pharmacy reports to be emailed to the pharmacy manager at regular intervals by configuring report subscriptions under **Admin → Reports → Scheduled Reports**.
</Tip>
