Whether you are a receptionist registering new patients, a doctor recording consultation notes, a lab technician processing test results, or an administrator generating reports, eHealthSystem brings all your workflows into a single, unified system. Use this documentation to get up and running quickly and to make the most of every feature the platform offers.

## Getting Started in Four Steps

Follow these steps to begin using eHealthSystem from day one:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to eHealthSystem">
    Open your clinic's eHealthSystem URL in a web browser and enter the username and password provided by your system administrator. On your first login, you will be prompted to set a new password before proceeding to the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register Your Patients">
    Navigate to **Patients → Registration** from the sidebar. Enter the patient's demographic details — name, date of birth, contact information, and any insurance or identification numbers — then save the record to generate a unique patient ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Visits and Clinical Workflows">
    Add the patient to the **Visits Queue**, record triage vitals, and assign them to the appropriate clinician. Doctors and nurses can then document consultation findings, request laboratory tests, and issue prescriptions — all from within the patient's visit record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process Billing and Close the Visit">
    Once the consultation and any pharmacy dispensing are complete, navigate to **Billing** to generate an invoice covering consultation fees, lab charges, and medication costs. Record the payment and mark the visit as closed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
