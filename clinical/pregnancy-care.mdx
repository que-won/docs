The Pregnancy Care module supports the full maternal care journey, from booking the first antenatal visit through delivery and postnatal follow-up. Each pregnancy episode is linked to the patient's medical record, giving clinicians a single, chronological view of gestational history, screening results, and risk factors across visits.

## Registering a Pregnancy

Open the patient's record from **Patients**, then select the **Pregnancy Care** tab and click **New Pregnancy**. Capture the booking details:

- **Last menstrual period (LMP)** and calculated estimated due date (EDD)
- **Gravida** and **parity**
- **Blood group and rhesus status**
- **Known risk factors** (hypertension, diabetes, previous caesarean, multiple gestation)
- **Booking weight, height, and BMI**

The system uses the LMP to auto-calculate the EDD and current gestational age on every subsequent visit.

## Antenatal Visits

Record each antenatal visit as a scheduled encounter under the active pregnancy episode. A visit captures:

| Section | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Vital signs | Blood pressure, weight, pulse, temperature |
| Obstetric examination | Fundal height, fetal lie and presentation, fetal heart rate |
| Investigations | Urinalysis, haemoglobin, HIV, syphilis, glucose screening |
| Interventions | Tetanus toxoid, iron and folate, IPT for malaria, deworming |
| Plan | Next appointment date, referrals, patient education notes |

<Tip>
Flag any visit where blood pressure, proteinuria, or fetal growth falls outside expected ranges. Flagged visits appear on the maternal risk dashboard for follow-up.
</Tip>

## Delivery Record

When the patient delivers, open the pregnancy episode and click **Record Delivery**. Complete:

- **Date, time, and place of delivery**
- **Mode of delivery** (spontaneous vaginal, assisted, caesarean)
- **Duration of labour** and complications, if any
- **Newborn details** for each baby: sex, birth weight, Apgar scores, resuscitation
- **Maternal outcome** and immediate postpartum notes

Recording the delivery automatically closes the antenatal phase and opens the postnatal phase of the episode.

## Postnatal Follow-Up

Postnatal visits are scheduled at standard intervals (typically 24 hours, 6 days, 6 weeks). For each visit, document:

- Maternal recovery, blood pressure, and any complications
- Breastfeeding status and infant feeding advice given
- Family planning counselling and method chosen
- Newborn assessment, immunisations, and growth monitoring

## Closing a Pregnancy Episode

Once the final postnatal visit is complete, click **Close Episode** on the pregnancy record. The episode remains visible in the patient's history and contributes to their gravida and parity on any future pregnancy.
