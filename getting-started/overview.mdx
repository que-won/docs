**eHealthSystem** is an integrated clinic and hospital management platform designed for healthcare facilities of every size — from small outpatient clinics to large multi-department hospitals. It serves every member of your care team: **receptionists** who register patients and manage appointments, **doctors** who record consultations and issue prescriptions, **nurses** who capture triage vitals and monitor wards, **pharmacists** who dispense medications and manage inventory, **lab technicians** who process diagnostic requests and post results, and **administrators** who oversee operations, configure the system, and generate reports. All these roles work within the same shared environment, so information flows seamlessly from registration through to billing without duplication or data loss.

## Core Modules

## Role-Based Quick Reference

Your access and default views in eHealthSystem depend on the role your administrator has assigned to your account. The table below summarises what each role is responsible for within the system.

| Role | Primary Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Receptionist** | Register and search patients, manage the visits queue, schedule appointments, and handle basic billing queries. |
| **Doctor** | Review patient history, record consultation notes and diagnoses, order lab tests, issue prescriptions, and approve referrals. |
| **Nurse** | Capture and update triage vitals (blood pressure, temperature, weight, etc.), monitor patient progress, and assist with ward management. |
| **Lab Technician** | Receive and action test requests, record sample collection times, enter results, and flag critical values for immediate clinician review. |
| **Pharmacist** | Review and dispense prescriptions, manage medication inventory, process drug requests, and record stock adjustments. |
| **Administrator** | Configure system settings, manage user accounts and role assignments, oversee all modules, and generate comprehensive facility reports. |

<Note>
  User roles and their associated permissions are configured exclusively by your system administrator. If you believe your role is incorrect, or if you need access to a module that is not visible on your dashboard, contact your administrator to request an account update.
</Note>
