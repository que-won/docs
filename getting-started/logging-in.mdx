Accessing eHealthSystem requires a personal user account created by your facility's system administrator. Your administrator will provide you with a unique username and a temporary password before your first login. All access is browser-based — you do not need to install any software. Simply open a supported web browser, navigate to your clinic's eHealthSystem URL, and authenticate with your credentials to reach your personalised dashboard.

## Logging In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Clinic's eHealthSystem URL">
    Launch a supported web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge are recommended) and type your facility's eHealthSystem address into the address bar. Your system administrator will have provided this URL — it typically follows the pattern `https://yourclinic.ehealthsystem.com`. Press **Enter** to load the login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Username and Password">
    On the login page, type your assigned **username** into the Username field and your **password** into the Password field. Both fields are case-sensitive, so ensure your Caps Lock key is off before typing. If you are logging in from a shared workstation, verify that no previous user's credentials are pre-filled in the fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Login' to Access the Dashboard">
    Click the **Login** button to submit your credentials. eHealthSystem will verify your details and, if correct, redirect you to your role-based dashboard. If your credentials are incorrect, an error message will appear — double-check your username and password and try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change Your Password on First Login">
    If this is your first time logging in, eHealthSystem will immediately prompt you to set a new password. Enter your current temporary password in the **Current Password** field, then type and confirm a new password of your choosing. Your new password must be at least eight characters long and should include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Click **Save** to apply the change and proceed to your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password Recovery

eHealthSystem does not currently offer a self-service password reset link on the login page. If you forget your password or your account becomes inaccessible, contact your **system administrator** directly and request a password reset. Your administrator can reset your password from the User Management section of the Administration module and provide you with a new temporary password. You will be prompted to change this temporary password the next time you log in.

## Session Timeouts

For security, eHealthSystem automatically ends your session after a period of inactivity. When your session expires, you will be logged out and redirected to the login page. Any unsaved work in progress may be lost, so save your records regularly. The inactivity timeout period is set by your system administrator and may vary between facilities. To end your session intentionally before the timeout, click your profile name in the top-right corner of the dashboard and select **Logout**.

<Warning>
  Never share your login credentials with anyone — including colleagues. Each user account is tied to an individual's actions in the system for accountability and audit purposes. If you suspect your credentials have been compromised, contact your system administrator immediately to have your password reset.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting Common Login Issues

If you encounter problems logging in, use the guidance below to identify and resolve the issue.

**Incorrect username or password**
Verify that you are typing the correct username (check for extra spaces or typos) and that Caps Lock is not enabled. Remember that both fields are case-sensitive. If you have recently had your password reset, use the new temporary password provided by your administrator.

**Account locked**
After a number of consecutive failed login attempts, eHealthSystem may lock your account as a security measure. If you see an "Account locked" message, contact your system administrator to have your account unlocked before attempting to log in again.

**Page not loading or blank screen**
Ensure you have a stable internet connection and that you are using a supported browser. Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then reload the page. If the issue persists, try opening eHealthSystem in a different browser or an incognito/private window to rule out browser extension conflicts.

**Redirected back to login after entering credentials**
This may indicate a browser cookie issue. Make sure your browser is configured to accept cookies from your eHealthSystem domain. Disabling any aggressive privacy or ad-blocking extensions temporarily may also resolve this.

**"Your account is inactive" message**
Your account may have been deactivated by an administrator. Contact your system administrator to confirm your account status and have it reactivated if necessary.
