The dashboard is your central hub in eHealthSystem — the first screen you see after logging in and the place you return to between tasks. It gives you an at-a-glance overview of your facility's current activity, surfaces the metrics most relevant to your role, and provides direct navigation links to every module you have access to. Understanding the dashboard layout will help you move efficiently through the system and keep up with the demands of a busy clinic or hospital.

## Dashboard Layout

The eHealthSystem interface is divided into three distinct areas: the **top navigation bar**, the **sidebar menu**, and the **main content area**.

### Top Navigation Bar

The top navigation bar runs across the full width of the screen and contains:

- **Facility name and logo** — displayed on the left, confirming which eHealthSystem instance you are logged into. This is especially useful in multi-facility deployments.
- **Notifications bell** — click the bell icon to view system alerts, such as critical lab results awaiting review, low drug stock warnings, or pending approvals relevant to your role. Unread notifications are indicated by a badge count.
- **User profile menu** — displayed on the right with your name and role. Click it to access your profile settings, change your password, or **Logout** to end your session securely.

### Sidebar Menu

The collapsible sidebar on the left side of the screen is your primary navigation tool. It lists all the modules you have permission to access, grouped by functional area. Common sections include:

- **Dashboard** — returns you to the home screen from anywhere in the system.
- **Patients** — links to patient registration, search, and records.
- **Visits / Queue** — opens the daily visits queue and triage management screens.
- **Clinical** — access consultation notes, prescriptions, and referrals.
- **Laboratory** — manage lab requests and post test results.
- **Pharmacy** — review prescriptions and manage dispensing and stock.
- **Billing** — create and manage invoices and payment records.
- **Reports** — generate and export operational and clinical reports.
- **Administration** — configure system settings and manage users (administrators only).

Click any menu item to navigate directly to that module. On smaller screens, use the hamburger icon at the top of the sidebar to collapse or expand it and reclaim screen space.

### Main Content Area

The large central area of the dashboard displays **summary cards**, **metric tiles**, and **quick-action shortcuts**. This is where the real-time snapshot of your facility's activity lives. The content adapts based on your assigned role, so what you see here is tailored to the tasks most relevant to you.

## Key Metrics and Summary Cards

The dashboard's summary cards give you an immediate pulse on facility activity without navigating to individual modules. The following metrics are displayed:

| Metric Card | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Patients** | The cumulative number of registered patients in the system. |
| **Today's Visits** | The number of patient visits registered or completed today. |
| **Pending Lab Results** | Lab test requests that have been submitted but whose results have not yet been posted. |
| **Pending Prescriptions** | Prescriptions issued by clinicians that are awaiting dispensing at the pharmacy. |
| **Outstanding Bills** | Invoices that have been generated but not yet settled by patients or insurers. |

Click any metric card to drill down into the corresponding module and view the full list of records behind that number.

## Quick Navigation via the Sidebar

The fastest way to move between modules is to use the sidebar menu. Each item is a direct link — a single click takes you to the relevant screen without any intermediate steps. To return to the dashboard at any time, click **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar or click your facility's logo in the top navigation bar.

If the sidebar is collapsed, hover over it or click the expand icon to reveal the full module labels alongside their icons.

<Tip>
  Use the **search bar** at the top of the sidebar (where available) to instantly find a patient by name, phone number, or patient ID. This is faster than navigating to the Patients module and running a manual search — particularly useful during busy clinic hours when you need to pull up a record quickly.
</Tip>

## Role-Based Dashboard Views

The metrics, quick-action buttons, and module links visible on your dashboard depend entirely on the role assigned to your account. For example:

- **Receptionists** see the visits queue status, today's appointment count, and quick links to patient registration and billing.
- **Doctors and nurses** see pending consultations, patients awaiting triage, and recent lab results flagged for their review.
- **Lab technicians** see outstanding test requests and recently posted results.
- **Pharmacists** see pending prescriptions awaiting dispensing and drug stock alerts.
- **Administrators** see a comprehensive overview spanning all modules, including financial summaries and user activity.

If a module is not visible on your sidebar or dashboard, you do not currently have permission to access it. Contact your system administrator to request a role update if you require access to additional functionality.
